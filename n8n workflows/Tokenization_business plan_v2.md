# Business Model Overview
The business model centers on enabling the tokenization of real-world assets through a blockchain-powered platform that leverages fiduciary legal frameworks, primarily targeting the Mexican and broader LATAM capital markets. Revenue generation is driven by multiple complementary streams including project setup fees ranging from $20,000 to $100,000, commissions on fundraising between 0.75% and 3%, and transaction fees on wallet transfers. Additional income arises from subscription services, premium regulatory reporting, and specialized tokenization services for collateral and certification processes. This diversified financial structure ensures resilient monetization aligned with customer activity and platform scalability.

At its core, the platform facilitates private capital raises by connecting two primary customer segments: asset issuers such as mining operations, real estate developers, and fund managers, and sophisticated accredited investors seeking new investment opportunities. Tokenized securities reflecting economic rights like dividends and profit-sharing are issued under fiduciary structures based on Mexican SAPI and trust (fiduciamientos) legal instruments, fostering trust, compliance, and security. The platform’s phased technological development begins with a white-label solution, evolving towards a proprietary Ethereum EVM-compatible infrastructure featuring integrated wallet management, KYC/AML compliance, escrow services, and liquidity solutions.

Customer relationships are carefully calibrated to support both issuers and investors across the capital raising lifecycle. Issuers benefit from streamlined project setup, legal compliance, and access to a targeted investor base, while investors gain transparent, liquid, and regulated token ownership options, enhancing market accessibility. Subscription services and premium reporting deliver ongoing value and regulatory transparency, strengthening engagement and platform stickiness. This dual-sided value proposition is instrumental in driving platform adoption and revenue growth.

Operationally, the platform acts as a secure, compliant intermediary leveraging innovative blockchain technology and fiduciary law to reduce traditional capital raising frictions such as illiquidity, slow processes, and high complexity. By embedding legally recognized fiduciary trusts into token issuance, the model creates a robust, scalable foundation for capital market disruption in Mexico and LATAM, with clear plans for phased geographical expansion into the USA, Europe, and Asia. This strategic approach aligns revenue mechanisms, legal structures, technology evolution, and customer segmentation into a coherent, investable business framework.

# Legal Structure
### 2.2 Legal Structure

The company is legally structured as a Mexican Sociedad Anónima Promotora de Inversión (SAPI), a flexible corporate form well-suited for innovative fintech ventures pursuing private capital raises. The SAPI entity combines the benefits of a traditional corporation’s limited liability with enhanced governance adaptability. This flexibility allows the company to tailor shareholder rights, voting mechanisms, and capital issuance terms to the unique needs of its tokenized investment projects. Registered in Mexico, the SAPI framework supports both domestic operations and international ambitions, forming a solid legal foundation for growth across Latin America and beyond.

Complementing the SAPI corporate form is the use of fiduciary trusts (fideicomisos) to hold and manage the underlying real assets that back the issued tokens. These trusts serve as a critical legal layer that separates and protects investors’ economic rights — such as dividends and profit shares — while maintaining compliance with Mexican securities law. By leveraging fiduciary structures, the company ensures legal clarity and asset security, reinforcing investor confidence. The trust arrangement also streamlines transparency and governance by imposing fiduciary duties on trustees responsible for asset stewardship and operational execution.

In terms of regulatory compliance, the company prioritizes robust frameworks covering Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) protocols directed at accredited investors. This approach aligns with both Mexican securities regulation and international best practices, enabling secure, compliant private capital raises via tokenized offerings. The company continuously monitors evolving legal landscapes in key markets such as the United States, Europe, and Asia, preparing a phased rollout that mitigates jurisdictional risk. This regulatory vigilance is essential to support the planned geographic expansion while safeguarding operational integrity and investor protection.

Overall, the integration of the SAPI entity with fiduciary trusts and a strong compliance regime creates a differentiated legal structure that directly addresses the business’s strategic objectives. It balances innovation with prudence by combining corporate governance flexibility, legal asset protection, and rigorous oversight. This architecture not only underpins the company’s pioneering role in digital tokenization of real assets but also builds a scalable and trustworthy platform for global capital formation.

  # Product/Service Description
The company offers a comprehensive tokenization platform designed to transform real-world high-value assets into compliant digital securities using blockchain technology. This end-to-end solution enables asset owners such as mining enterprises, real estate developers, and investment funds to launch new investment projects that adhere fully to regional securities regulations. By leveraging Ethereum-based token standards—ERC-20 for fungible tokens and ERC-3643 for permissioned, regulated tokens—the platform supports the creation, issuance, and management of asset-backed digital tokens that represent enforceable economic rights in the underlying assets.

Central to the platform’s functionality is a modular architecture that optimizes the issuer and investor experience. The issuer dashboard guides project creation, token issuance, and capital raise management, integrating essential KYC/AML investor onboarding workflows to ensure regulatory compliance. Investors access a dedicated portal enabling wallet connectivity, secure transaction monitoring, and streamlined participation in private token sales limited to accredited investors. Escrow services are integrated to safeguard funds during subscription rounds, while planned secondary market readiness facilitates liquidity once tokens are issued. This holistic approach ensures asset digitization, investor transparency, and capital raising efficiency within a secure legal framework.

A distinctive feature of the platform is the legal infrastructure embedding fiduciary trusts (“fiduciamientos”) under the Mexican SAPI corporate framework. This structure underpins token ownership by legally segregating economic rights linked to dividends, profit distributions, or cashflow streams inherent to the tokenized assets. By coupling blockchain-based token ownership with enforceable fiduciary legal rights, the platform offers heightened investor protection, regulatory acceptance, and market confidence. This innovative fusion of technology and fiduciary law differentiates the offering from conventional security token solutions and is foundational to the platform’s multi-jurisdictional market expansion strategy.

Technologically, the platform employs standardized Ethereum token protocols to ensure compatibility, security, and compliance automation. The use of ERC-3643 enables permissioned tokens that restrict transferability to approved investors, satisfying diverse regulatory requirements. The architecture supports wallet integrations popular among institutional and retail investors, providing ease of use and secure custody options. The phased development plan starts with deploying a white-label solution for rapid market entry and validation, progressing to a proprietary system that incorporates advanced features such as automated compliance checks, flexible fund management, and secondary liquidity mechanisms aligned with evolving regulatory frameworks.

Complementary to the core platform, the company offers ancillary services including fiduciary trustee roles, ongoing regulatory reporting support, escrow management, and subscription-based issuer services. These enhance operational efficiency, legal compliance, and investor relations for issuers navigating the complexities of multi-jurisdictional tokenized capital raises. By delivering both a technologically robust platform and expert fiduciary/legal services, the company ensures a comprehensive solution that meets the stringent demands of regulated capital markets while enabling issuer innovation and investor access to previously illiquid asset classes.

# Break-Even Analysis

This delineation allows for a clear focus on contribution margin per project when assessing profitability dynamics.

Thus, to cover the fixed costs of $1.2 million exclusively through project fees, the platform needs to successfully onboard and close approximately 15 projects annually. In summary, the business expects to achieve operational profitability by maintaining disciplined cost management and realizing a steady ramp-up in project volumes, reaching the critical volume of 15 projects per year by year three. Between years three and five, the strategy shifts toward scaling revenue streams, expanding the issuer pipeline, and refining profitability. During this period, efforts concentrate on elevating EBITDA margins, enhancing proprietary intellectual property, and broadening market reach to target jurisdictions such as the USA and Europe. These efforts not only increase enterprise value but also prepare the company for more complex liquidity events by demonstrating sustained profitability and market traction.

The final phase, spanning years five to seven, centers on positioning the company for exit opportunities. This includes initiating acquisition discussions with strategic buyers, exploring potential mergers, or assessing readiness for an IPO or tokenized IPO if market conditions and regulatory clarity permit. Trigger milestones guiding this phase include achieving annual revenues above $20 million, sustaining EBITDA profitability over 12 months, and securing robust compliance certifications. The ability to adapt this timeline to emerging market trends, regulatory changes, or funding availability is critical, ensuring that execution remains aligned with external conditions.

Throughout all phases, milestone tracking and periodic strategic reviews are essential to maintain alignment with exit readiness indicators. Cross-functional teams—from finance to legal and investor relations—play pivotal roles in monitoring KPIs, updating valuation models, and engaging with potential partners. This dynamic approach supports responsive decision-making, enabling acceleration or recalibration of exit plans as market opportunities evolve.

In essence, this timeline blends structured preparation with strategic flexibility, safeguarding value maximization while accommodating shifting regulatory landscapes and competitive dynamics. It enables a thoughtful, data-driven progression toward liquidity events that fulfill investor expectations and sustainable company growth.

# Success Metrics for Exit
Key performance indicators (KPIs) for measuring exit readiness must directly reflect the company’s strategic growth targets and investor value creation. Financially, achieving an annual recurring revenue (ARR) exceeding $20 million is a foundational benchmark, demonstrating market traction and revenue scale. Complementing this is sustaining an EBITDA margin above 20% over a minimum 12-month period, signaling profitability and operational efficiency attractive to acquirers or public markets. Additionally, cumulative capital raised through projects surpassing $50 million indicates strong capital market confidence and successful platform utilization, underpinning valuation assumptions.

User engagement and platform effectiveness also represent critical success metrics. The active accredited investor base should number above 5,000, providing sufficient liquidity and market depth to support scalable tokenized asset transactions. Completion of at least 20 tokenized projects showcases platform maturity and validates the business model in real-market conditions. Platform reliability, measured by uptime greater than 99.9% combined with robust security audits, ensures operational credibility which is vital for investor assurance and compliance adherence.

Intellectual property and regulatory compliance KPIs focus on long-term value preservation and exit simplicity. The platform’s proprietary technology must be fully developed, operational, and protected by active IP registrations, providing defensible technological barriers to entry. Full legal compliance without material regulatory warnings across key jurisdictions consolidates the company’s reputation and facilitates smoother transaction processes in acquisition or IPO scenarios. Strategically, an established market presence in at least two major investment jurisdictions outside LATAM—such as the USA and Europe—demonstrates cross-border scalability and regulatory sophistication.

Market and investor relations metrics further enhance exit attractiveness. The company must evidence ongoing engagement with potential strategic partners and acquirers, indicated by pipelines of discussions or letters of intent, which can trigger premium valuations. Investor satisfaction and retention rates should remain high, reflecting confidence in business fundamentals and growth prospects. Positive analyst reports and favorable market sentiment reinforce valuation credibility, easing investor and board approval for exit execution.

Collectively, these KPIs form a coherent dashboard integrating financial strength, market penetration, operational stability, intellectual property security, compliance robustness, and investor confidence. Attaining and surpassing these metrics signals a high readiness level for exit opportunities, whether through acquisition, strategic merger, tokenized IPO, or secondary market transactions, ensuring that the company maximizes valuation multiples and achieves well-defined revenue and profitability thresholds aligned with its business-specific growth trajectory.
